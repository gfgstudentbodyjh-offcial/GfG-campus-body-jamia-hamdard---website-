• CodeMania Hackathon 2026 (7th–9th September 2026)
   The CodeMania Hackathon 2026 was organized by Kickr Technology, with the GeeksforGeeks Campus Body, Jamia Hamdard serving as the Community Partner. Held from 7th to 9th September 2026, the hackathon provided students with a dynamic platform to develop innovative solutions for real-world challenges while enhancing their technical and problem-solving skills. Participants collaborated in teams, received guidance from experienced mentors, and competed for an exciting prize pool of ₹2,50,000, along with internship, networking, and career opportunities. The event successfully promoted innovation, teamwork, entrepreneurship, and practical learning, inspiring students to transform their ideas into impactful technological solutions.
